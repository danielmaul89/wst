FIELD REPORTCase Study

Built for compact utility vehicles.

The XWD-48 replaces a heavier, slower-charging power system inside the same chassis footprint — engineered to withstand vibration, moisture and temperature swings across a full working season.

01 / Challenge

Same footprint. More working time.

The compact chassis left no room to grow. The new battery had to preserve every mounting point while providing more usable energy across a full outdoor shift.

02 / Engineering

The system was designed from the machine inward.

A modular 48V LFP architecture, sealed enclosure and integrated i-BMS were developed as one system around vibration, thermal protection and service access.

03 / Validation

Built to repeat the result, every shift.

Electrical verification, environmental testing and production controls turn the validated prototype into a traceable battery system ready for dependable series delivery.
