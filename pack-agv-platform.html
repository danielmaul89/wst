How the pack is put together.

Shaped to the vehicle

The case is long and slim because that is the space an AGV has: under the deck, between the wheels. Terminals and the communication connector come out of the top face, where the vehicle harness reaches them.

Padded against a working day

EVA padding lines the ends and sides and an internal divider braces the middle, so a pack that is docked and undocked several times a shift is not held together by its fasteners alone.

Serviced without opening the pack

A separate service lid gives access to the connector and terminal plates while the main lid stays sealed, so routine work on the vehicle never breaks the seal on the cells.
