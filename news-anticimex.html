Anticimex fights rodents intelligently and poison-free

At WS Technicals, we are glad to support Anticimex in their work with intelligent and more sustainable pest control solutions.

Success Story Anticimex

Anticimex is one of the world's leading companies within pest control and prevention, and they've actually been at it since 1934. Back then, the company started as a family business joining forces in the fight against bed bugs. In fact, the name Anticimex literally means "against bed bugs" in Latin.

Today, Anticimex works proactively with prevention, modern technology, and effective solutions against all kinds of pests. For Anticimex, pest control is not only about solving an immediate problem, it's also about protecting people, the environment, and surrounding ecosystems through a smarter and more preventive approach.

An electronic, poison-free trap

And this is where WS Technicals comes into the picture. One example from Anticimex's extensive portfolio of pest control solutions is the Smart Box, an electronic rodent trap designed to eliminate rats and mice without the use of poison. When a rodent enters the trap, it's instantly eliminated using electricity, after which the trap automatically resets and prepares for the next capture. At the same time, notifications are sent directly to Anticimex's monitoring system, allowing activity to be registered and action to be taken quickly.

This battery-powered and poison-free approach eliminates the need for rodenticides and helps reduce the risk of harming other animals and wildlife. At the same time, the solution supports a more targeted and preventive pest control strategy, where data and digital monitoring play a central role.

Batteries built for stability and long service life

WS Technicals supplies the lithium batteries used in the Anticimex Smart Box. The batteries are developed with a strong focus on stability and long service life, ensuring reliable operation over extended periods of time. This is an important part of a solution that needs to remain active, dependable, and ready to respond whenever activity occurs.

We're pleased to deliver a small but quite important component to Anticimex's vision for the future of pest control: intelligent, poison-free, and focused on both prevention and environmental responsibility.
