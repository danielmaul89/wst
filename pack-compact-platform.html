How the pack is put together.

Cells, held rather than packed

21700 cylindrical cells sit in moulded holders that space them apart and carry them through shock and vibration. The holders set the gaps the design needs between cells, so nothing depends on how tightly the pack was assembled on the day.

Current through busbars

Busbars tie the cells into their series and parallel groups and carry the pack current out to the terminals. Sense and power lines run back to the board on one harness, so the wiring is a single part to fit and a single part to check.

A board that watches every cell

The BMS board handles protection and cell balancing, and rides inside the lid where it is reachable without disturbing the cell stack. The main casing takes the structural load, and the lid seals the whole assembly.
