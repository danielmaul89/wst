How the pack is put together.

A string in a rack unit

GridPower modules sit side by side in one series string across the width of the unit, with FR4 sheets insulating them from the sheet metal above and below. Rack brackets carry the whole weight on the frame rather than on the shell.

Air moved where it is needed

Fans on their own bracket pull air across the string and out of the rear panel, so the unit holds its rating in a closed cabinet with equipment above and below it.

Read from the front

The indicator board sits behind the front panel and reports state of charge and fault status at eye height in the rack. Service happens from the front too: the panel and top sheet come off without pulling the unit out of the cabinet.
