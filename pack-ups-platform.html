How the pack is put together.

Built for the rack, not the shelf

FR4 sheets insulate the build from the sheet metal above and below it, and rack brackets carry the whole weight on the cabinet frame rather than on the shell. The model here is the production enclosure: structure, airflow, front panel and wiring.

Air moved where it is needed

Fans on their own bracket pull air through the unit and out of the rear panel, so it holds its rating in a closed cabinet with equipment above and below it.

Read from the front

The indicator board sits behind the front panel and reports state of charge and fault status at eye height in the rack. Service happens from the front too: the panel and top sheet come off without pulling the unit out of the cabinet.
