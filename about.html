About WS Technicals

At WS Technicals, we believe the strongest battery solutions are created through close partnerships, shared knowledge and a genuine willingness to solve difficult problems together. We provide customized industrial-grade batteries by beginning with the work each product must do instead of starting with a catalogue solution.

Since our start in 2015 as a spin-off from the intra-logistics sector, collaboration has shaped the way we work. We listen before we design. We share what we know. And we remain closely involved from the first requirements conversation to a stable, production-ready system.

Our role is not simply to supply a battery. It is to understand what your product must achieve and bring the engineering, BMS expertise and manufacturing discipline needed to make that ambition dependable in the real world.
