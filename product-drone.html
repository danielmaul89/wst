Case Study · Drones

Every gram traded for flight time.

An inspection drone only earns its keep in the air. When our customer needed longer missions without redesigning the airframe, the answer was not a bigger battery. It was a denser system built to swap in seconds between flights.

In real operation

Built around the work the product must do.

Inspection crews measure a drone by how much ground it covers before it needs to land. The battery therefore has to balance flight time, payload, safe handling and fast turnaround as one system.

01

The operating challenge

A fixed airframe and payload leave little room for additional battery mass.

02

The engineered response

Cell chemistry, packaging and connector design are matched to the aircraft and its mission profile.

03

The practical result

Predictable flight time and rapid battery changes help the crew keep moving between inspections.
