The Story

A pack built around the mission, not a part number.

Inspection crews measure a drone by how much ground it covers before it needs to land. That meant the pack had to fit inside a fixed weight budget, work with the existing bay, and change over fast enough to keep a crew moving between sites.

01

Every gram traded against flight time

The airframe's weight budget was fixed — more energy meant a denser cell chemistry, not a bigger, heavier pack.

02

High energy-density Li-ion, quick-swap connector

A 6S Li-ion pack matched to the gimbal and flight-controller load, with a keyed connector so field crews can swap packs without tools.

03

More flight time, faster turnaround

Longer missions per battery, and a pack swap fast enough to keep an inspection crew moving between sites.
