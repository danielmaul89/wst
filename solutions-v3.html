What custom means

The battery follows the application, never the other way around.

Potential solutions are evaluated as one system. Performance, safety, integration and production economics remain connected from the beginning.

01

Requirements baseline

Duty cycle, environment, interfaces, safety targets and commercial constraints are agreed before the architecture is committed.

02

System architecture

Cell chemistry, packaging, thermal strategy, BMS, charging and machine interfaces are engineered as one complete system.

03

Engineering validation

Representative prototypes are verified electrically, thermally and mechanically in the intended customer application.

04

Production engineering

Tooling, process controls, traceability and end-of-line testing turn the validated design into a repeatable manufacturing system.

05

Compliance release

Required testing, technical documentation and market approvals are completed around the final battery configuration.

06

Series production

Controlled sourcing, serialized traceability and end-of-line verification protect quality throughout production and product life.
