What custom means

The battery follows the application—not the other way around.

Potential solutions are evaluated as one system. Performance, safety, integration and production economics remain connected from the beginning.

01

Duty cycle

Energy, peak power, charge windows and expected lifetime are balanced against how the machine actually works.

02

Physical environment

Geometry, temperature, vibration, ingress protection and service access shape the mechanical system.

03

Machine integration

BMS, CAN communication, connectors and control logic are engineered around your platform.

04

Release and scale

Compliance, end-of-line testing, traceability and manufacturability are resolved before production.
