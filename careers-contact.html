The role

WS Technicals designs and builds complete lithium battery systems for machines that have to keep working — agricultural robots, lifting equipment, industrial vehicles and stationary storage. Every system is developed around one specific application, so the electronics are never picked off a shelf and dropped in.

As Electronics Engineer you own that part of the system. You size and configure the battery management, design or specify the power path and the interfaces to the machine, and build the test setups that show the design behaves as intended before it reaches a customer. You work alongside mechanics, software and production in the same building, which means decisions are made together and quickly rather than handed over.

What you will be doing

  • Specify and configure BMS platforms for new packs, from cell monitoring and balancing to protection limits and fault handling.
  • Design and review the power path: contactors, pre-charge, fusing, current measurement, charging and service disconnect.
  • Define the electrical interface to the machine — CAN communication, wake-up and charging signals, connectors and harnesses.
  • Build and run test setups for characterisation, validation and end-of-line testing, and document what they show.
  • Support the safety and compliance work: risk assessment, the relevant standards and the documentation that goes with them.
  • Follow the design into production, so the boards, harnesses and test steps are realistic to assemble at volume.

What we are looking for

  • A degree in electrical or electronic engineering, or equivalent experience.
  • A few years of practical work with electronics in a product context — battery systems, power electronics, automotive or industrial equipment.
  • Comfort with schematics, measurement equipment and a bench: you would rather verify something than assume it.
  • Working knowledge of CAN and embedded interfaces, and enough software fluency to talk properly with the people writing it.
  • Danish or English, written and spoken. Our working language is Danish, but the team and our customers work in both.

Useful, but not required

  • Hands-on experience with lithium cells, pack safety, or transport and certification requirements.
  • Familiarity with functional safety work, or with EMC testing and how to survive it.
  • PCB design, or experience steering an external design partner through one.

What we offer

A small, senior team where your work goes into machines you can point at. Projects run from first concept to series production, so you follow your own decisions all the way through instead of handing them to another department.

Terms are agreed individually and reflect your experience. We are based in Aarhus, with the workshop, test bench and production in the same building as the desks.
