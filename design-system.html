Glass system

Depth without visual noise.

Glass is reserved for controls, contact surfaces and selective overlays. Reflection should describe the surface, not become an effect of its own.

Material surface

Controlled transparency.

A subtle top reflection, restrained blur and enough opacity to preserve WCAG contrast.

Rules

  • Never reduce text contrast to create glass.
  • Use one top-edge highlight, not a full bright border.
  • Keep lighting slow and tied to the pointer.
  • No hover translation on buttons.
  • Prefer depth from layered opacity over heavy shadows.
Forms

Clear, calm and credible.

Fields remain visibly distinct from the dark surface while keeping the glass treatment controlled and readable.

Accessibility

Contrast is a design constraint.

These checks are calculated from the live token values. Normal text should reach 4.5:1; large text should reach 3:1.
